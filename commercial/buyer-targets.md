# Buyer target definition — AI Acceptance Assurance

**Status:** target hypothesis, not a verified prospect list.

## Initial segment

B2B SaaS companies that are deploying or procuring third-party AI systems for customer-support ticket classification and routing.

## Buyer roles

- Head / Director of Customer Support Operations
- VP Customer Experience / Customer Operations
- AI Automation Lead
- Support Systems / CX Technology Lead
- Procurement owner for AI-enabled customer operations

## Trigger events

- Selecting a new AI support supplier.
- Comparing two or more candidate systems.
- Moving an AI workflow from pilot to production.
- Supplier model, prompt, workflow or configuration update.
- Material increase in support volume or new support categories.
- Quality incident that prompts re-validation.

## Why this segment is the initial hypothesis

Ticket routing has observable outputs and buyer-defined expected outcomes. The first product slice can test whether the system selected the correct queue/category and returned required structured fields. This is narrow enough to build and inspect without claiming to evaluate every aspect of support quality.

## Disqualifiers

- No external AI supplier or no meaningful system change.
- No buyer-owned test cases or expected outcomes.
- No ability to export structured outputs.
- Existing platform/process already handles acceptance and change control adequately.
- No recurring test trigger or budget owner.
- Data access cannot be approved safely.

## Research status

No organisations are yet confirmed buyers. Build a prospect list only after identifying public evidence that a company has deployed or is actively procuring AI support automation. Confirm the buyer role and trigger before outreach.
