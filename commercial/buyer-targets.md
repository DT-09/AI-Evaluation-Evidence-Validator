# Initial buyer and channel targets

Research date: 2 November 2026.

These organisations publicly describe technical or AI due-diligence services. They are **prospects for discovery or specialist-partner discussions**, not confirmed buyers. Their public service pages do not establish a willingness to outsource or purchase this assessment.

The first message should ask about one workflow limitation, not pitch the tool as a replacement for their diligence practice.

## Qualification questions

- Do your current engagements include AI model-performance or benchmark claims?
- What evidence do you receive to verify those claims?
- Can your existing process independently reconstruct reported metrics from row-level predictions and labels?
- Which checks are routinely manual or difficult to reproduce?
- Would a specialist evidence package be useful as a subcontracted workstream, or is this already fully covered?
- What would need to be true for you to commission a small, paid pilot?
