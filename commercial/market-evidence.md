# DealTie market evidence and positioning constraints

## Direct overlap
- NarrEx publicly describes reading a pitch deck/CIM/management presentation alongside the underlying financial model, checking claims against model figures, and providing source evidence. It lists advisor/preparer and due-diligence modes. https://www.narrex.app/faq
- Kroll offers financial due diligence and QoE for buyers, sellers, lenders and investment banks. https://www.kroll.com/en/services/transaction-advisory-services/financial-due-diligence
- Hansen Advisory offers QoE and sell-side preparation/pressure-testing. https://hansenadvisoryservices.com/services

## Implication
Do not market DealTie as unique, first, proprietary, AI-powered, audit-grade, or fully automated. The MVP's defensible claim is narrower: analyst-confirmed observations, deterministic comparison, source references and an exportable exception register.

## Commercial hypothesis
The first customer is more likely a small advisory team with recurring CIM/QoE/model preparation or review than a large bank. The initial sale should be a paid, tightly scoped second-pass tie-out—not a software subscription.

## Evidence still missing
- A buyer has the problem.
- The problem occurs often enough to matter.
- Existing review steps are insufficient.
- A budget owner will pay for this specific output.
- The product saves time or catches meaningful issues without adding review burden.

No customer validation or willingness to pay is established.
