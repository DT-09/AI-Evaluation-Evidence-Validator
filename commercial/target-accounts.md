## Qualification questions

1. Does the team compare CIM, QoE and model figures on each mandate?
2. Which metrics and periods are checked before circulation?
3. Where are source references, reviewer notes and unresolved differences recorded today?
4. Which part of the process is still manual or repeated?
5. Does the current process leave a material evidence-traceability gap?
6. Is there a near-term mandate or revised pack that could serve as a bounded paid pilot?
7. Who owns the workflow and can approve external review support?

## Qualification rule

These are research candidates, not leads. A candidate becomes qualified only after a relevant owner confirms a recurring workflow problem, describes the current alternative, identifies a plausible budget/approval route and agrees to discuss a paid scope.

Do not infer pain, budget or willingness to pay from a service page. Do not claim these firms are customers, partners or interested buyers.

Any outreach, contracting, invoicing, payment, tax, confidentiality and client-data arrangements must be handled by an appropriately authorised adult/business representative. Qualified finance professionals must supervise substantive financial interpretation.
