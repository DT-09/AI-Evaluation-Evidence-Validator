## Qualification questions
1. Does the team reconcile CIM, QoE and valuation-model figures repeatedly?
2. Which metrics and periods are checked on every transaction?
3. Where are source references and reviewer notes recorded today?
4. What errors or delays escape the existing process?
5. Would a source-linked exception register save enough analyst/reviewer time to justify a paid pilot?
6. Who owns the workflow and can approve a small paid trial?

Do not count a public contact page, an unanswered message or a vendor case study as a lead. A qualified opportunity requires a reply from a relevant owner, a specific workflow pain, a plausible budget owner and agreement to discuss a paid scope.
