# DealTie — buyer discovery pipeline

**Product:** pre-issue financial evidence tie-out across a CIM, QoE report and valuation model.  
**Initial buyer:** partner, director or senior associate at a boutique M&A advisory or financial diligence firm.  
**Status:** no verified prospects contacted; no buyer, pilot or revenue is claimed.

## Target account profile

Prioritise firms that:
- prepare or review sell-side transaction materials;
- produce CIMs and valuation models, or coordinate QoE providers;
- run lean deal teams where analysts manually reconcile headline metrics;
- complete multiple mandates per year;
- can approve a small, bounded external review without enterprise procurement.

Avoid broad corporate finance lists with no evidence of relevant workflow ownership. A firm name alone is not a qualified prospect.

## Roles to qualify

| Role | Why they matter | Qualification question |
|---|---|---|
| Managing partner / M&A partner | Owns delivery quality and client risk | Who signs off on consistency across the final document pack? |
| Director / VP | Often supervises deal execution | How are cross-document tie-outs reviewed before circulation? |
| Senior associate | May own the manual workflow | Which checks still happen in spreadsheets or by repeated manual review? |
| Financial diligence lead | Understands QoE source data | How are QoE figures reconciled to the CIM and model? |

## Research fields for each account

Record only publicly available business information:
1. Firm and official website.
2. Relevant service line (M&A advisory, financial diligence, transaction services).
3. Publicly documented role and current title of the contact.
4. Evidence that the firm prepares or reviews CIM/QoE/model packs.
5. Public business contact route or professional profile.
6. Why the workflow is plausibly relevant.
7. Verification date and source URLs.
8. Outreach status and actual response.

Do not infer that a firm has the problem or budget. Mark unknown fields as unknown.

## Qualification gates

A prospect is qualified only after confirming:
- the person participates in or owns transaction-pack review;
- the workflow includes cross-document financial tie-outs;
- manual effort or an unresolved evidence-traceability gap exists;
- the problem recurs or has an upcoming trigger;
- the buyer can describe the current process and its existing tools;
- there is an authorised route to discuss a paid, bounded pilot.

A positive reply, profile view or unanswered message is not a qualified conversation.

## Commercial next step

Offer a non-confidential scoping discussion. Do not request transaction files, financial figures, client names or deal identifiers. Start with a synthetic example or a redacted schema.

Any contract, fee, payment, confidentiality or client-data arrangement must be handled by an appropriately authorised adult/business representative. Qualified finance professionals must supervise substantive financial interpretation.
