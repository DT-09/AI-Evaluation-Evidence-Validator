## Qualification before an interview counts

Confirm that the person:
1. currently holds the stated role or has moved into an equivalent customer-operations decision role;
2. personally owns or materially influences AI support acceptance, supplier evaluation, QA or change approval;
3. has evaluated or materially changed a third-party AI support system;
4. can describe a recent concrete example;
5. can identify the existing tools and budget owner.

If a prospect is not the decision owner, ask for an introduction to the operational owner. Do not count a referral or an unanswered message as an interview.
