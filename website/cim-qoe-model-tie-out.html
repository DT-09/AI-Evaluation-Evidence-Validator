M&A financial document tie-out

Check the CIM against the QoE and valuation model—before circulation.

DealTie supports a bounded, analyst-supervised consistency review of selected headline figures across a transaction document pack. It records the metric, period, reported value, stated unit and source location, then exposes differences for qualified human review.

The task

Reconcile the same agreed metrics across the documents that tell the deal's financial story.

Confidential Information Memorandum

Capture selected headline figures and preserve the relevant page reference. DealTie does not determine whether the CIM's presentation is correct.

Quality of Earnings report

Compare analyst-confirmed observations for the agreed metric and period. Definitions and accounting treatments still require reviewer confirmation.

Valuation model

Record reported cell references and compare stated values. The prototype reads cell values; it does not audit formulas or workbook logic.

Exception handoff

Show the reported values, observed spread, comparison status, contributing source rows and unresolved questions in one review package.

A narrow, explicit workflow

What happens in a scoped review.

  1. Agree up to 15 metrics, periods, up to three source documents, unit conventions and tolerance.
  2. Extract candidate text and cell values from supported files.
  3. Have an analyst confirm the metric, period, value, unit and source reference.
  4. Apply deterministic comparisons to the confirmed observations.
  5. Review the exception register, resolve open questions and export the evidence package.
Important: A mismatch is an investigation lead—not proof of an error, misstatement, loss or recoverable amount. DealTie does not decide which source is authoritative.
Initial engagement boundary

One pack. Up to 15 metrics. Up to three source documents.

Included

  • Analyst-confirmed observation ledger.
  • Source-linked exception register.
  • Value, unit, missing-value, duplicate-source and coverage classifications.
  • Open questions and limitations.
  • JSON, CSV and standalone HTML outputs.
  • One reviewer walkthrough and one correction pass.

Not included in the prototype

  • Scanned-PDF OCR or semantic metric matching.
  • Currency, scale or period conversion.
  • Workbook formula evaluation or accounting judgement.
  • Secure deal-room hosting, role permissions or audit-grade history.
  • Audit, QoE, valuation, legal or transaction opinions.

The public workbench is not a secure environment for confidential deal files. Begin with synthetic or properly redacted material. Any engagement, contracting, payment and data-handling arrangements require an appropriately authorised business representative and qualified finance supervision.
