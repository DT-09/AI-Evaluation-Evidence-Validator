The transaction review problem

One financial story. Several documents. No room for silent drift.

Headline figures are repeated across marketing materials, diligence reports and valuation models. A changed period, unit or value can leave reviewers chasing the source trail under deadline.

01 / VALUE CONSISTENCY

Revenue and EBITDA differ

A figure in the CIM may not match the QoE report or model. DealTie shows the reported values side by side and records the observed spread.

02 / UNIT DISCIPLINE

Units are not interchangeable

USD versus EUR, thousands versus millions, or different period labels require explicit reviewer attention. The prototype does not silently convert them.

03 / SOURCE TRACEABILITY

The reference gets lost

Each confirmed observation retains its document and page, worksheet or cell reference where extractable, so the reviewer can return to the source.

How the review works

A controlled path from source material to an exception register.

Extraction assists the analyst. No extracted candidate becomes a financial observation until its meaning and source are confirmed.

01 / INGEST

Load the source pack

Selectable-text PDFs, Excel workbooks or a structured CSV evidence ledger enter the review workflow.

02 / CONFIRM

Map each observation

Confirm the metric, period, value, unit and source reference before comparison.

03 / RECONCILE

Expose exceptions

Apply deterministic tolerance checks and flag value, unit, missing-value, duplicate-source and coverage issues.

04 / HAND OFF

Preserve the evidence

Inspect the contributing source rows and export JSON, CSV and a readable HTML review report.

The current prototype does not infer metric equivalence, source hierarchy, currency conversion, accounting treatment or which reported figure is correct.

Scope and boundaries

Know exactly what is included—and what is not.

Clear boundaries are part of the product. DealTie should be evaluated on traceability and reproducibility, not inflated claims.

Initial paid review scope

  • One authorised, non-confidential or appropriately redacted document pack.
  • Up to 15 agreed headline metrics across up to 3 source documents.
  • One agreed period map, unit convention and comparison tolerance.
  • Confirmed observation ledger and source-linked exception register.
  • Open questions, exclusions, JSON/CSV/HTML outputs, one walkthrough and one correction pass.

Current prototype limitations

  • No scanned-PDF OCR or semantic metric matching.
  • No automatic currency, scale or period conversion.
  • No workbook formula evaluation or accounting interpretation.
  • No secure deal-room hosting, role permissions, multi-user adjudication or audit-grade history.
  • No audit opinion, QoE conclusion, valuation opinion or transaction recommendation.

The public workbench is a prototype, not a secure transaction environment. Do not upload confidential deal files. Any engagement, contracting, payment and data-handling arrangements require an appropriately authorised business representative. Qualified finance professionals must supervise substantive financial interpretation.

Questions buyers should ask

Specific answers before a pilot is scoped.

The best fit is a team that needs a bounded consistency review and can provide an authorised, redacted or synthetic source pack.

Who is DealTie designed for?

Boutique M&A advisory firms and financial diligence teams preparing or reviewing transaction materials. The operational user is typically an analyst or associate; the buyer is likely a partner, director or diligence lead. This is an initial buyer hypothesis, not a claim of validated demand.

What does the paid review cover?

The initial scope is one document pack, up to 15 agreed headline metrics and up to three source documents: a CIM, QoE report and valuation model. It includes an analyst-confirmed observation ledger, exception register, open questions, exports, one walkthrough and one correction pass. Scope and fee are agreed before work begins.

Does DealTie decide which number is correct?

No. It compares analyst-confirmed observations under explicit rules. It does not determine source authority, accounting treatment, metric equivalence or the correct figure. A mismatch is an investigation lead, not proof of an error or loss.

Can we upload confidential transaction files?

Not to the current public prototype. It does not provide production-grade authentication, role-based access, secure deal-room controls or audit-grade history. Begin with synthetic or properly redacted material and obtain written approval for any authorised data-handling arrangement.

Is DealTie an audit or a Quality of Earnings engagement?

No. It is a narrow consistency-review workflow. It does not replace an audit, QoE engagement, accounting advice, valuation work, legal advice or transaction diligence.

How do we start?

Use the non-confidential inquiry form to describe the workflow at a high level. It is a request for discussion, not an engagement or data authorisation. An appropriately authorised business representative must agree the scope, fee, terms and data-handling route.
